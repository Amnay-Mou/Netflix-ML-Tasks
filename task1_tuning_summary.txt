Weights: 41 weight settings were tried on 300 titles and the best one was checked on 500 different titles (genre overlap@10, genres hidden): hand-picked 0.3369 vs tuned 0.3369, gain +0.0000 (95% range +0.0000 to +0.0000). There is no clear gain, so the hand-picked weights are kept.

The genre weight (3.0) stays a judgment call. The brief asks for suggestions 'based on genres, categories and content attributes', so genres lead; and it cannot be tuned against genre overlap because that would be circular. Sensitivity: with genre weight 1 only 60% of the suggested titles are the same as with 3, with weight 5 it is 96%. A non-circular way to justify it is the human review: have reviewers compare lists made with different genre weights.

Several liked titles (300 simulated users per situation): 3 titles, same genre: hit share, each in turn minus average = +0.027 (95% range +0.008 to +0.046): 'each in turn' is clearly better. 3 titles, same genre: genre coverage, each in turn minus average = +0.077 (95% range +0.035 to +0.119): 'each in turn' is clearly better. 3 titles, 3 different genres: hit share, each in turn minus average = +0.109 (95% range +0.080 to +0.138): 'each in turn' is clearly better. 3 titles, 3 different genres: genre coverage, each in turn minus average = +0.220 (95% range +0.188 to +0.252): 'each in turn' is clearly better.

If 'each in turn' is clearly better when the liked titles are from different genres, say so and offer it as the better way to combine very different picks; if there is no clear difference, keep the simple average.
